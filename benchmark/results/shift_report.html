Method

6,000 spectra of six plastics from FTIR-Plastics (Villegas Camacho et al., 2024, CC BY 4.0), read as ATR. Today compares each spectrum at one position. Tolerant tries shifts of -8 to +8 cm⁻¹ and each reference keeps its best score. The verdict rule is the same. False probable is measured with every reference of the true material removed. HDPE and LDPE both count as PE.

Looked at after the results

No rule or setting depends on these.

  • 264 of the tolerant search's 298 false probables, and all 264 of today's, are spectra labelled PS. 300 spectra with that label have no polystyrene ring band at 700 cm⁻¹ (median height 0.003, against 0.33 in the spectra called PS) and have a polypropylene methyl band at 1376 cm⁻¹ (0.14, against 0.008). Their r is 0.97 to PP and 0.07 to PS. They are probably polypropylene with the wrong label. The protocol has no rule for this, so they stay in and rule 1 stands.
  • The other 34 false probables are new. They are PVC spectra in the 8 cm⁻¹ table, and today's search gives no false probable on PVC. In a sample of 300 PVC spectra the tolerant search called them PS at r 0.95.
  • PET lost right answers: 248 today, 188 tolerant. Every other material kept or gained them.
  • With a band shift of 2 or 4 cm⁻¹ the tolerant search gave the same answers as at shift 0, in right and not right.

Limits

  • All spectra come from one instrument and six common plastics, so this says nothing about other materials or instruments.
  • The record does not say whether the 500 sample numbers are 500 physical pieces, and the numbers repeat across the two resolutions. The intervals treat the spectra as independent and may be too narrow.
  • The applied shifts are computed, not measured on an instrument that drifted. Shifts of 2 and 4 cm⁻¹ land exactly on positions the search tries, which flatters it: a shift between two tries would score a little lower.
  • False probable has one reference set removed at a time, so it tests these six plastics only.
  • These figures say nothing about real fab samples.
